### QUERY method: update

Commands stay `POST`; queries and streams move to `QUERY` (`21a1ece5c`). There is no `POST` fallback, and the wrong
method is `405 MethodNotAllowed`.

Tooling: `sync-platform-api.py` accepts the `query` method, so 40 generated pages now reference `QUERY`. Mintlify
validates the spec and renders these pages with a `QRY` sidebar pill and a `curl --request QUERY` sample. The
playground was not tried against the live API.

### Transaction types: update

New `Trade` type: one asset leg of a spot fill. `Settlement` is now profit or loss realised on a contract, and
`Conversion` is limited to conversions outside the order book. Paper posts a spot fill's two legs as `Trade`, and its
transactions sum to its wallet totals per asset.

Pages: `concepts/hosts`, `capabilities/host-capabilities`, `guides/portfolio`, `paper/{overview,going-live,fees-and-funding}`.

### Accounts search and fee override labels: update

List accounts takes `search`. `PaperFeeOverride` requires `label`.

Pages: `concepts/accounts`, `paper/fees-and-funding`.

### Web app: update

The Terminal is the home screen. New accounts start from the host with a real or paper choice. Portfolio is replaced
by an account page (Overview, Balances, Positions, Open Orders, Transactions, Fills). A paper account's fee tier can be
chosen in the app. The chart shows the traded price only. The order book gains grouping and a choice of sides, and the
Terminal toolbar shows connection health.

Pages: `platform/overview`, `platform/add-exchange`.
