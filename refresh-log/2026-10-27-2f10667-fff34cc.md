### Every operation needs an API key: changed content

The `public` badge tag and the per-operation `security: []` overrides are gone; venue operations, List hosts and List
host markets now return 401 without a key.

Pages: `authentication` (public operations section removed, intro, tenant/account section), `quickstart` (host
discovery curls send the key), `concepts/venues-and-hosts`, `concepts/hosts`, `concepts/venues`,
`capabilities/{overview,host-capabilities,venue-capabilities,order-rules}`, `guides/market-data`, `guides/orders`
(key headers on every curl), `api-reference/platform/{overview,conventions,errors}` (domain table, auth, order of
checks).

### Streams are HTTP JSON Lines: rewritten content

One `POST` per topic; the `200` is `application/jsonl` with an `itemSchema` message union. No Subscribe/Unsubscribe,
topic limits or close codes. Account events lost `topic`. Errors before the stream are ordinary responses; every
stream ends with `Ended`; blank keepalive lines at least every 15 seconds; a slow client is cut without `Ended`.

Pages: `guides/streams` (rewritten and retitled "Streams", same path; only inbound anchor `#transaction-events` kept),
`how-plurex-works`, `index`, `api-reference/platform/{overview,conventions,errors}`, link labels in `quickstart`,
`guides/orders`, `guides/portfolio`.

Tooling: `scripts/sync-platform-api.py` no longer reads AsyncAPI. Stream pages are ordinary OpenAPI pages with
`playground: "none"`. Every page now leads with a Query, Command or Stream badge derived from the path (`KINDS`);
`snippets/operation-badges.mdx` colours them. `snippets/capability-check.mdx` lost its stream variant: an unsupported
stream is `422 NotSupported` like any call. `AGENTS.md` and `README.md` updated; market data streams removed from the
"do not document" list.

### Venue market data streams: new content

Eight streams: order books, trades, prices, best quotes, statistics, indicative funding, open interest, candles. Each
starts with a `Reset` and then sends `Update`s. Venue capabilities gain `subscribeTo…` leaves; market descriptors gain
`subscribe` flags. New `EXTRA` rows give each stream page its market descriptor.

Pages: `guides/market-data` ("Follow market data live", "Keep a book", trades, values and candles),
`concepts/venues` (Streams section), `capabilities/venue-capabilities` (leaves, descriptors, resolve table, examples),
`capabilities/overview`, `guides/streams`.
