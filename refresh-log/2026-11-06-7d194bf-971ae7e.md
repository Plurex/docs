## Topics

### Paper leverage and liquidation: update, new page

Paper perpetuals now take a per-market leverage inherited from the model host, hold and lock margin at it, and are
liquidated at maintenance margin. Maintenance is half the initial margin at the market's max leverage. A pool past it
has its orders cancelled and its largest position closed by a reduce-only market order marked `origin: Liquidation`. A
flat pool worth less than nothing before weights is written off to zero as an `Adjustment`. Withdrawals check the pool.

New page `paper/leverage-and-liquidation`, which also takes over the collateral detail from `paper/overview`. It owns
the topic; other pages link to it. Added to `docs.json` and the `AGENTS.md` site map.

Pages: `paper/{overview,going-live,fees-and-funding}`, `how-plurex-works`, `index`.

Public from upstream's accepted limits: risk tiers, later or worse closes at high leverage, that a position can stay
open past its liquidation price while paper stands down, and spot buys not checking margin. Left out as internal: pass
intervals, price age limits, the screening and consistency reads, order ID derivation, and the design rationale.

The maintenance rule is stated as a formula, as funding is. It is a term the reader needs to plan around, and
`totalMaintenanceMargin` reports the live value. Raise it if it should stay implied.

Removed, no longer true: "paper has no leverage", "there is no liquidation", and "markets the model host stops listing
stop accepting new orders", which upstream now contradicts. An opening order can still be accepted on such a market, so
the sentence was cut rather than replaced.

Corrected in passing: "a negative balance blocks new risk" was too strong. The capacity calculation subtracts debts at
full value, so an account with other collateral can still open. Upstream's AGENTS.md uses the stronger wording; the code
wins, and the mismatch is in `agent_plans/spec_feedback_for_plurex.md`.

### Margin groups, positions and `origin`: update

`AccountMarginState` is breaking: `baseCollateralAsset` and `accountMarginRatio` are gone, replaced by `marginGroupId`,
`valueAsset` and `maintenanceMarginUsage`, and `totalMaintenanceMargin` is nullable. `Position.liquidationPrice` and
`Order.origin` are new required fields. An instrument can appear in more than one List open positions item, and
GetAccountMarketState returns summed sides. ListAccountMarginStates can return `422 NotSupported` for an account margin
mode.

Pages: `guides/{portfolio,orders,streams}`, `quickstart`, `concepts/hosts`, `capabilities/overview`. Every JSON example
with these shapes was checked against the spec schemas.

### Paged history: update

Bybit transactions and fills now reach all retained history behind one cursor, so a quiet stretch can be an empty page
with a `nextCursor`. A transaction in a delisted market has no `instrument`.

Pages: `api-reference/platform/conventions`, `guides/portfolio`.

### Feed outages on paper: update

Paper waits rather than decides during an outage: resting orders and triggers carry on from the first fresh data, and
missed fills and crossings are not invented. An order whose book cannot be read is rejected with `502 UpstreamFailure`.

Pages: `paper/overview`.

### Reduce-only market orders below minimum size: update

Matching skips size filters on a reduce-only market order, so a remainder below the minimum closes by requesting at
least the minimum and being trimmed. A request for exactly the remainder fails order validation.

Pages: `paper/overview`.

Pages: `platform/overview`.
