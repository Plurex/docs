## Topics

### Deleting an account

A paper account is deleted at once: its open orders are cancelled, its positions closed at market, and its balances go
with it. A real account is forgotten with its connection, and everything on the exchange stays. The old claim that
deleting breaks history records is gone, as the app and spec no longer make it.

Pages: `platform/accounts` (warning), `platform/add-exchange` (Remove an account), `concepts/accounts`.

### Members have only their own access

Members no longer read every account by default: a member has only what the owner gives them, as a key does. The app
shows only what the access allows: account actions (Rename, Delete, Deposit, Update connection) and New Account are
hidden otherwise, and the Terminal shows a view-only note instead of the order form on an account the member cannot
trade.

Pages: `platform/organisations`, `platform/accounts`, `platform/terminal`.

### Two-factor setup key

The setup dialog shows the key as text with a copy button, for apps that cannot scan. Page: `platform/settings`.

### Not documented

can grant.
